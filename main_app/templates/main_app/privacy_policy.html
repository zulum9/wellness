Privacy Policy

App: The Mindful Queen (com.mindfulqueen.app)

Organization: Beyond Survival Sisterhood

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

The Mindful Queen provides mental health support resources, emergency helpline links, and daily encouragement. We do not collect sensitive personal medical records. Account data (such as email addresses) is collected strictly for account management and security.

2. How Data Is Used

We use collected data solely to deliver wellness content, maintain account authentication, and facilitate access to community support services.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or lease user data to third parties. Data is only processed to maintain app functionality and host cloud infrastructure securely.

4. Account and Data Deletion

Users have full control over their personal data. To request account and data deletion, visit our Account Deletion Request Page.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at your support email address.
