Account & Data Deletion Request

App: The Mindful Queen (com.mindfulqueen.app)

Developer: Beyond Survival Sisterhood

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your account and associated personal data from The Mindful Queen, you can request deletion using either of the following steps:

  1. In-App Deletion: Log into the app, go to Settings / Profile, and select Delete Account.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your registered account to your support email with the subject line Account Deletion Request.

Types of Data Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following information is permanently removed from our active databases:

  • Account profile details (name, email address, password hash)
  • Saved user preferences, journal entries, and saved resources
  • Authentication tokens and session logs

Data Retention

Most account data is deleted immediately or within 30 days of your request. System activity logs are automatically purged after 90 days for fraud prevention and security monitoring.
